      Parent Studio — Create & Import Quizzes

      Create quizzes manually, or upload photos of Honza's textbook pages in Gemini chat to generate ready-to-import study packs.

      📸 How to turn Textbook Photos into Quizzes

      1. Take a photo of 1–4 pages from Honza's textbook, workbook, or school notebook.
      2. Drop the photo right here into our Gemini / Antigravity chat and say: "Make a quiz for Honza from this page".
      3. I will automatically add the study notes, flashcards, and mixed quiz questions directly into your app (or give you a JSON block to paste via "Import Quiz JSON").
      Copy AI Prompt for external Gemini / ChatGPT chats
